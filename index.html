A note from the CEO

Madam Oforiwaah Adwoa Amanfo

Chief Executive Officer, Vinos Farms

I love growing vegetables and raising poultry. To young people who are curious about farming, my advice is to start small and learn from someone who works the land. A vegetable bed or a few hens can teach you a great deal. Agriculture can help you earn a living, feed your community and strengthen Ghana’s economy.
